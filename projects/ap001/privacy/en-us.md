# Privacy Policy for "Alchemist & Phial Spirit"

**Last Updated: March 6, 2026**

Welcome to **Alchemist & Phial Spirit** (hereinafter referred to as "the Service"), a Discord game bot developed by **Goo Co., Ltd.** (hereinafter referred to as "the Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data related to your Discord account.

---

## 1. Scope of Data Collection
To maintain game operations and process transactions, we collect only necessary data:
* **Basic Identification**: Discord User ID, Server ID (Guild ID), and Channel ID.
* **Game Progress**: Levels, Experience Points (XP), virtual inventory, potion recipes, Phial Spirit status, and related interaction logs.
* **Transaction Information**: When you make payments (e.g., AP refills or subscriptions), we record Order IDs, purchased items, amounts, timestamps, and payment status.
* **What We DO NOT Collect**: The Service **never** accesses, stores, or processes your credit card numbers, bank account details, or other sensitive payment credentials. All payments are handled externally by PayPal.

## 2. Use of Information
Collected data is used solely for the following:
* **Service Provision**: Ensuring your game progress and "Phial Spirits" are correctly saved and loaded.
* **Transaction Verification**: Validating payment status and delivering purchased digital items or membership benefits.
* **Operational Optimization**: Adjusting game balance, debugging, and preventing fraudulent activities or cheating.

## 3. Third-Party Data Processors
Your data may be processed by the following services:
* **Discord (Platform)**: Basic account data is governed by [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
* **PayPal (Payment)**: Payment processing is handled by PayPal. Your financial information is protected under [PayPal's Privacy Statement](https://www.paypal.com/webapps/mpp/ua/privacy-full).
* **Google Cloud (Infrastructure)**: Our application and databases are hosted on Google Cloud Platform.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
* **Game Data**: To allow players to resume progress, game data is not automatically deleted when the bot is removed from a server.
* **Financial Records**: According to tax and financial regulations, records related to transactions (such as Order IDs) will be retained for the legally required period (typically 5-7 years), even if a user requests data deletion.
* **Deletion Requests**: If you wish to completely remove your personal game data, please contact the development team (see Section 8).

## 5. Age Restrictions
The Service strictly adheres to the age requirements set by **Discord's Terms of Service**.
